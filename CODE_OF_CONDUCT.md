# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be rigorous, welcoming, and safe for people preparing for a demanding role.

Be respectful. Critique claims and reasoning, not people. Correct errors with evidence. Do not shame candidates for experience gaps or language proficiency. Do not pressure anyone to share confidential interview content. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these standards, the source policy, or the prohibition on proprietary material.
